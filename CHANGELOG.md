## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-08

### Added
- Complete A–Z alphabet interactive curriculum designed for early childhood learners.
- High-contrast, large-format letter cards with uppercase and lowercase recognition.
- Native offline Text-To-Speech audio pronunciation for letter names, phonics sounds, and object words.
- 26 custom-crafted animated 2D vector illustrations (Apple, Bear, Cat, Duck, Elephant, Fish, Giraffe, House, Ice Cream, Juice, Kite, Lion, Monkey, Nest, Orange, Penguin, Queen Crown, Rainbow, Sun, Train, Umbrella, Van, Whale, Xylophone, Yo-yo, Zebra).
- Interactive tap-to-bounce motion and playful auditory cues (pops and chimes).
- Bilingual learning support for English and Bengali (বাংলা) translations and phonetics.
- Auto-Play guided slideshow mode for hands-free preschool learning.
- 26-Letter adaptive grid view for freeform discovery across phones and tablets.
- "Listen & Find" pressure-free letter listening game with encouraging positive reinforcement.
- Parents' Corner protected by a Designed for Families / COPPA compliant parental gate challenge.
- Centralized `AdConfig` with official Google test AdMob identifiers and COPPA child-directed parameters.
- Custom adaptive launcher icons and store assets (512x512 app icon, 1024x500 feature graphic, phone & tablet screenshots).
- Comprehensive legal and store metadata package.

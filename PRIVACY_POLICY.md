## 1. Introduction

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of young children and families who use **ABCoo** ("the Application"). ABCoo is an early-learning Android application designed to teach the English alphabet from A to Z through playful visual sequences, speech pronunciation, and gentle interactions.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information. Because our application is designed specifically for children under the age of 13, we comply strictly with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **Google Play Families Policy**.

---

## 2. Information We DO NOT Collect

We believe in maximum privacy for children. As such:
- **No Personal Information:** We do NOT collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, photographs, biometric data, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No User Accounts:** The application requires no account registration, login, profile creation, or social media integration.
- **No Geolocation:** We do NOT access, track, or collect device GPS coordinates or precise location data.
- **No Audio Recording:** The application uses device-internal Text-to-Speech (TTS) playback only. We do NOT record, monitor, or upload any microphone audio or voice data.
- **No Analytics / Tracking:** We do NOT integrate third-party behavioral analytics, telemetry SDKs, or session-recording trackers.

---

## 3. Offline-First Functionality & Local Storage

ABCoo is designed to operate completely **offline**. All educational content—including the 26 alphabet letters, phonics sequences, words, illustrations, and pronunciation audio—runs locally on the user's Android device without requiring internet access.

Any user preferences (such as language mode toggles or sound states) are stored strictly locally in non-volatile memory on your device and are never transmitted to any external server.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

Protecting young children's safety online is paramount:
1. **Zero Profiling:** We never build profiles or track children across sessions or other applications.
2. **Parental Gate:** Access to parent-facing settings, external links, and publisher information is protected by a parental gate (an adult math challenge) in full compliance with the Google Play Families Policy.
3. **No In-App Purchases:** The application does not contain in-app purchases, microtransactions, or subscription paywalls.

---

## 5. Advertising & Google AdMob

The application integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) under the Google Play Designed for Families Program:
- All advertising requests are strictly configured with:
  - `TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT = TRUE` (COPPA Compliance)
  - `MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING = "G"` (General Audiences only)
  - Non-personalized advertising only (zero interest-based tracking or behavioral profiling).
- Banner and interstitial advertisements are placed exclusively at natural pause/transition points and never disrupt active toddler touch interactions, games, or letter tracing.
- Parental gates protect any publisher links or external actions.

---

## 6. Device Permissions

ABCoo operates with minimal permissions. It requests **zero dangerous or runtime permissions** (no Camera, no Microphone, no Contacts, no Storage, no Location). All features run out-of-the-box immediately upon installation.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ABCoo does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers, there is no personal data retained or shared. If you uninstall the application, all local cache and preference files stored on your device by the Android OS are automatically erased.
